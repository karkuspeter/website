My research interests include autonomous driving and robotics, with a focus on end-to-end vision-language-action and world-action models, closed-loop simulation, and RL post-training. At NVIDIA, I work on Alpamayo, an open platform for reasoning-based vehicle autonomy, focusing on closed-loop evaluation and training of driving models. I am a core contributor to Alpamayo models, AlpaSim, and AlpaGym. My earlier work introduced differentiable algorithm networks for robot planning, navigation, mapping, and localization.
